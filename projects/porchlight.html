A little place
A light left on

Porchlight · iOS app · in testing

A private app for a friend group and the places they share, from the lake house to the padel court.

Launching in the first half of 2027

Who's at the lake this weekend?

Every group of friends has its places: someone's lake house, a few flats, the café, the court. But nobody knows who's where right now. In the group chat the question gets lost, and sharing your location everywhere is far more than anyone wants.

Porchlight sits in between. It's a light in the window that says I'm here, come by, and only at the places your group shares. Each place becomes a small world of its own, and the owner decides who gets to look in.

From a written spec to a working world.

Designed and built with AI coding agents, from a written spec. SwiftUI handles the interface, RealityKit renders each place as a small diorama, and iCloud keeps every group's places private and in sync, with no server of my own.

The sky over each place follows the real sun and weather there. Owners choose who can see their place and what they share.
