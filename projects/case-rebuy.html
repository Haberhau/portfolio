Do customers come back?

At work · Silhouette · Analysis · SQL views in SQLite

Customer records and sales from Salesforce, across two brands, one row per product. I built the analysis as reusable SQL views in SQLite, split every result by brand and sales channel, and wrote up the findings for the business. Most of the work was deciding which rows to trust.

Company-internal. This page shows how I worked; the results stay inside.

Three questions

Each answered per brand and per sales channel.

  • How long until a customer buys again?Time from one real purchase to the next.
  • What do they buy next?Frames again, or spare parts.
  • Who has quietly stopped?Customers grouped by time since their last order.

Cleaning

From raw rows to rebuys you can trust.

Each rule came from questioning a result that looked too good, or simply odd. Schematic: no real proportions.

The rules

What counts as a rebuy, and when a customer counts as lapsed.

All views are plain SQL in SQLite, so every number can be traced back to the rows behind it.
