Say what's on your mind
Marginalia finds the next step

Marginalia · iOS app · in testing

A notebook you talk to. Dictate a thought the way it comes out, and Apple's on-device model sees what's in it: a task, an event, a reminder. You decide what to add.

Launching in the first half of 2027

Runs entirely on the phone, on Apple's built-in AI.

Talk first, sort later.

Most to-do apps want you to sort things while you're still thinking. Marginalia lets you just talk: you dictate, the words appear as you say them, and when you're done it reads the whole thing and suggests what to do with it.

A web illustration of the flow: the transcript and the suggestions are scripted, and no speech recognition or language model runs on this page.

The intelligence stays on the phone.

Everything happens on the phone. Apple's on-device Speech framework writes down what you say as you say it, and the language model built into Apple Intelligence turns the finished note into structured suggestions. Both ship with iOS, so there's no AI service to pay for, no account and no backend.

It works offline, and nothing lands in your calendar or reminders until you confirm it. Built with AI coding agents.
