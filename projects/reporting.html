From inbox to dashboard, automated

At work · Silhouette · Data analyst since 2024

The sales numbers used to arrive as a stream of emailed spreadsheets and were pulled together by hand, once a week. I automated the path from the inbox to the dashboards: the numbers now arrive on their own, every day.

Company-internal. This page shows how it works; the internal figures stay inside.

The problem

Every week, someone copied the figures from a pile of emailed spreadsheets into the sales reporting. For months, that someone was me.

The ERP sends one spreadsheet per email. Pulled together by hand, the dashboards were only as fresh as the last copy session.

How it flows now

Many emails in, one automated step, the dashboards out.

What it does

  1. Catches every emailEach email carries one spreadsheet. Power Automate picks up every attachment as it arrives.
  2. Reads what mattersWhere a spreadsheet needs more careful handling, Python extracts just the relevant figures.
  3. Feeds the dashboardsThe numbers land where the Looker Studio dashboards read them, so they refresh without anyone touching them.
  4. Runs every dayWith no one in the loop, the update no longer waits for a weekly slot.

What changed

  • −90%manual work on the sales reporting.
  • Dailydashboards refresh every day instead of once a week.
  • FreshThe e-commerce team now makes its decisions on yesterday's sales, not last week's.
